Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Shipwreck Bar, 335 Gladstone Road, Gisborne, handles information in connection with this website. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. If you ring us or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number and email address, and we use them only to reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. Embedded services such as Google Maps and Google Fonts may collect standard technical data under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

Any contact details you share with us are used to answer your enquiry, take a booking or arrange a function. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Third party services

Links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google services take you to platforms we do not control. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Ring 06 867 3701 or email us and we will sort it out.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Ring us on 06 867 3701 or use the contact details on the home page.

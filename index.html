What's on at the Shippy

Seven days of cold drinks and good company, and a calendar that fills up fast from Thursday on.

Outdoors

The garden bar

A big covered garden bar out the back. Sun in the afternoon, lights and music after dark, and room for a full Saturday crowd.

Live

Live music

Local bands, touring acts and DJ sets, from old school rock nights through to reggae. Most weekends there's someone on.

Sing

Karaoke and open mic

The mic gets a workout most weeks. Karaoke nights, open mic sessions, and a crowd that backs anyone who has a go.

Sport

Big screens, pool and gaming

Catch the footy or the races on the big screens, rack up a game of pool, or duck into the gaming lounge.

On tap

Cold taps and a full bar

Cold beer by the jug or the handle, wine, RTDs and spirits, priced the way a local should be.

Book it

Functions and fundraisers

Birthdays, leaving dos, charity nights and club fundraisers. Book the garden bar or talk to us about the whole place.

Gladstone Rd

A local tavern that became the late night spot

Shipwreck Bar started out as a straight-up Gisborne tavern and grew into the place that stays up latest on Gladstone Road. Old school rock nights, charity fundraisers, twenty-firsts and quiet Tuesday handles have all rolled through the doors.

Locals just call it the Shippy. Come in for one after work, stay for the band, or book the garden bar for your next do.

  • Locally owned and run
  • Open seven days, late Tuesday to Saturday
  • Garden bar bookable for functions
